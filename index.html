Signature Keynote

TRUST PAYS™

Use Tech. Love People. Never the Other Way Around.

Ideal Audience
Executives, managers, HR and culture leaders, association members, and teams navigating AI and organizational change.
Value
A practical framework for adopting technology while strengthening the trust, expertise, and human judgment that make it useful.

You can buy the software. You can announce the strategy. You can give everyone a login. But you can’t automate trust.

When people don’t understand where they fit—or believe the next tool is being introduced at their expense—technology becomes more than an implementation challenge. It becomes a leadership challenge.

In TRUST PAYS™, Kimberly & Coach challenge the false choice between investing in technology and investing in people. The opportunity isn’t to make humans less necessary. It’s to make human expertise more powerful. Audiences learn to Start with And, Expertize Your People, and Master Over Time.

Takeaways

  • Start with And: Replace “technology or people” thinking with an approach that develops both.
  • Expertize Your People: Identify where AI can remove busywork and give human expertise more room to contribute.
  • Keep humans at the control panel: Preserve human judgment, accountability, and direction.
  • Master Over Time: Use the Orient, Support, Value, and Challenge cycle to sustain growth.
  • Choose a practical next step: Identify one change that can strengthen both capability and trust.

Sales Edition

TRUST SELLS

Use Tech. Love People. Close More.

Ideal Audience
Sales kickoffs, revenue leaders, sales managers, customer success teams, account managers, and channel partners.
Value
A people-first approach to using AI without losing the expertise, credibility, and customer connection that make a trusted advisor valuable.

Your competitors have access to AI, too. They can generate the emails. Summarize the calls. Build the presentations. So what makes your team worth choosing?

Nobody buys from a prompt.

When everyone can produce more, faster, the advantage isn’t simply more activity. It’s a better understanding of the customer—and the judgment to do something useful with it. Because faster isn’t always closer.

Takeaways

  • Recognize the difference between increasing activity and creating customer value.
  • Use AI to support preparation while keeping people responsible for accuracy and judgment.
  • Build expertise that makes customer conversations more useful.
  • Protect human connection when automation makes generic outreach easy.
  • Identify one way to become a more valuable trusted advisor.
